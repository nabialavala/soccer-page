All About Me

What was my motivation creating this?


I created this site to share my personal takes on soccer and hopefully promote some debates and discussions. One of my favorite parts about being a soccer fan is the banter, so I thought it would be fun to create a site where people can see some of my opinions on different players, teams, and topics. Soccer is also the biggest sport in the world, and following it has introduced me to different countries and cultures that I probably would have never learned much about otherwise. I want this site to be a place where I can talk about the sport from my own perspective while also learning more about it.

Who am i?


Like I mentioned before, I have loved soccer for most of my life and grew up playing it. Outside of soccer, I also really enjoy engaging in new and interesting experiences. I like learning about, doing, and trying things that I don't know much about because I think it's more fun to experience something new than to always stick with what I'm already familiar with. Whether it's traveling somewhere new, learning about another culture, or just trying something I've never done before, I'm usually open to the experience.
